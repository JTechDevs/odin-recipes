Description

My favourite dish is basically any form of Pasta, but fettucine alfredo will always have a special place in my heart. Nothing beats a nice bowl of pasta after a long day!

Ingredients

  • 200g fettuccine pasta
  • 2 tablespoons of butter
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 150ml double cream
  • 60g grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • A little pasta water (to thin the sauce)
  • Fresh parsley

Steps

  1. Cook the pasta: Bring a pot of salted water to a boil. Add the fettuccine and cook according to the packet instructions until al dente.
  2. Melt the butter: While the pasta cooks, melt the butter in a large pan over medium-low heat.
  3. Add garlic: Add the minced garlic and cook for about 30 seconds until fragrant. Don't let it burn.
  4. Make the sauce: Pour in the double cream and stir gently for 2–3 minutes.
  5. Add Parmesan: Stir in the grated Parmesan until melted and the sauce becomes smooth and creamy.
  6. Season: Add salt and black pepper to taste.
  7. Combine: Drain the pasta (save a little pasta water) and toss it into the sauce until fully coated.
  8. Adjust: Add a splash of pasta water if the sauce is too thick.
  9. Serve: Top with extra Parmesan, black pepper and fresh parsley if desired.
